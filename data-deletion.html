ACE Shot Diagrams — Delete Your Data

Last updated: 2026-10-21.

Delete individual diagrams

Sign in at codeonline.io/ace and use My Diagrams → the × button to delete a cloud diagram. Local diagrams live only in your browser: clear site data / local storage for codeonline.io, or use the browser's “Clear site data” control, to remove them.

Delete your account and all cloud diagrams

Email kevinelong@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Google or Facebook sign-in, with the subject “ACE account deletion”. Include the provider you used and the email address. We will delete the ACE account, its cloud diagrams, its collected copies, and the social-login link, then confirm by reply. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days; minimal records may be retained only where required by law.

Disconnect Google or Facebook

Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → ACE → Remove. Google: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services → ACE → Remove access. This disconnects sign-in. To remove ACE cloud diagrams as well, use the account deletion request above.

Shared links and collected copies

Unshare or delete a diagram to stop new visitors using its link. Diagrams other users have collected are independent copies in their libraries and remain there unless those users delete them. If a shared link must be revoked urgently, delete the source diagram and email us.
