ACE Shot Diagrams — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-21.

Accounts

Signing in is optional. Guests keep diagram saves in this browser's local storage. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, the provider shares your name, email address, and profile picture with ACE. We store that account data in PostgreSQL on the ACE server.

Diagrams and sharing

A cloud diagram stores its title and shot state (ball positions, shot settings, and annotations). Private diagrams are visible only to you. If you create an unlisted share link, anyone with that link can view the diagram while it remains shared; treat the link as a secret. Collecting a shared diagram creates an independent copy in your library. Deleting a diagram removes it from your library; copies collected by other users remain theirs.

Cookies

ACE uses a session cookie for sign-in and a CSRF cookie for API protection. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Third parties

Google and Facebook provide optional sign-in. We do not sell or rent account data or diagrams. The server and PostgreSQL database run on the ACE operator's infrastructure.

Retention and deletion

Delete individual diagrams from the library. To delete your account and all cloud diagrams, email kevinelong@gmail.com; we will remove the account and its diagrams. Guest local saves can be cleared from your browser.

Contact

Questions: kevinelong@gmail.com.
